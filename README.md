# 🌊 JALANVI AI — Autonomous Marine Mission Intelligence Platform

> **"Don't just ask the ocean a question. Give the ocean a mission."**

Built for the **ISRO Earth Observation & Marine Intelligence Challenge** (Smart India Hackathon 2026), **Marine AI** transforms raw satellite, oceanographic, meteorological, and geospatial observations into **explainable, safe, continuously adaptive marine decisions**.

## 🏆 The 5 Core Innovations

1. **🧠 Marine Brain & Dynamic State Model:**
   Maintains a living, spatio-temporal representation of the ocean (SST, Chlorophyll, PFZ, wave swell, wind vectors, currents, bathymetry, geofences) and vessel state.

2. **🚢 Mission Digital Twin & What-If Simulator (Flagship):**
   Converts queries into formal mission models. Users can simulate departure delays (`+2h`, `+4h`), fuel levels (`150L` to `450L`), and wave severity factors to dynamically recalculate **Safe Operating Windows**, **Safety Margins**, **Fuel Reserves**, and **Decision Stability**.

3. **🗺️ Marine Opportunity Surface:**
   Replaces crude "go to highest chlorophyll" logic with a multi-objective decision frontier balancing **Fishing Opportunity + Hydrodynamic Risk + Fuel Transit Cost + Geofencing Constraints**.

4. **⚔️ Adversarial Decision Intelligence (Red-Team Challenger):**
   Every recommendation generated by the Planner Agent is challenged by an Adversarial Red-Team AI ("What could trap or endanger this vessel?") before a formal **Decision Contract** is issued.

5. **🔄 Offline-First Marine Intelligence & Dead-Zone Predictor:**
   Predicts the 14 NM cellular dropout contour and generates a downloadable, self-contained **Offline Mission Pack** with cached nautical tiles, local rule-based safety guardians, and GPS dead-reckoning.

## 🤖 Agentic Multi-Agent Architecture (DAG)

Marine AI orchestrates specialized autonomous agents:
- **🎯 Intent & Multilingual Agent:** Natural voice and text decomposition across 8 Indian languages (Telugu, Tamil, Hindi, Bengali, Malayalam, Kannada, Gujarati, English).
- **🧭 Mission Orchestrator & Planner:** Hierarchical goal planning and candidate generation (Plan A, Plan B, Plan C).
- **🛰️ Satellite & Earth Observation Agent:** Ingestion of ISRO Oceansat-3 (OCM-3) chlorophyll gradients and INSAT-3DR thermal infrared SST fronts.
- **🌦️ Ocean & Weather Intelligence Agent:** High-resolution wave swell, wind vectors, and cyclone formation metrics from INCOIS SWH / ECMWF.
- **🛑 Geospatial & Geofencing Guardian:** Enforces real-time polygon barriers for the International Maritime Boundary Line (IMBL), naval live-fire firing arcs (NOTAM #092), and Coringa Marine Sanctuaries.
- **⛽ Route Optimizer & Fuel Guardian:** Hydrodynamic drag routing with guaranteed safe return fuel reserve.
- **⚔️ Adversarial AI Challenger:** Devil's advocate critic disproving unfeasible plans.
- **📜 Decision Guardian & Contract Validator:** Formulates the immutable Decision Contract with confidence, stability index, and hard abort thresholds.

## 🔍 Why / Why-Not Explainability

No black boxes. Every recommendation features:
- **Why This?** Specific empirical correlations (SST thermal front at 28.4°C, 1.45 mg/m³ chlorophyll bloom, 1.2m waves, 79% fuel reserve).
- **Why NOT the Alternatives?** Explains why Zone Bravo (highest catch opportunity) was disqualified due to afternoon wave swells exceeding 2.6m and fuel reserve falling below 15%.
- **Satellite Evidence Provenance:** Traces raw spectral telemetry → derived geophysical layers → thermal edge extraction → decision contract.
- **Counterfactual Reasoning:** Explains what parameter changes would alter the advice.

## 🧪 AI Marine Scientist

For scientific queries like *"Why has fish productivity declined in this coastal sector?"*, the platform generates ranked competing causal hypotheses (e.g., Warm-Core Eddy Upwelling Suppression vs Pelagic Baitfish Migration vs OMZ Intrusion) with supporting and contradicting empirical matrices.

## Deploy to Render

This repository includes a Render Blueprint (`render.yaml`) and a multi-stage `Dockerfile`. The Docker image builds the Vite frontend and serves it from the FastAPI application, so the UI and `/api` endpoints share one Render web service and origin.

1. Push this repository to GitHub and create a new Blueprint in Render from that repository.
2. Render reads `render.yaml`, builds the Docker image, and checks `/health` after deployment. `JWT_SECRET` is generated automatically.
3. Add any optional provider credentials (such as `OPENAI_API_KEY` or `GEMINI_API_KEY`) in the Render service environment if those integrations are needed.

The included free service uses SQLite on Render's ephemeral filesystem. User accounts and other database records can be lost when the service restarts or is redeployed. For persistent production data, attach a Render persistent disk, mount it at `/var/data`, and set `MARINE_AI_DB_PATH=/var/data/marine_ai.db` in the service environment.
